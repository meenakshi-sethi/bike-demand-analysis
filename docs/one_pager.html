Key findings

Temperature is the single biggest leverBy a wide margin the strongest predictor of daily demand.
Growth is real, not weather-driven2019 demand was structurally higher than 2018 independent of weather — the business is gaining organic traction.
Bad weather is costly, not just inconvenientLight snow/rain suppresses demand more than any other single factor drags it down.
Summer and winter both beat springSeptember and Saturdays add an extra demand bump on top of seasonal effects.

Recommendations

  1. Shift marketing spend toward September and the summer/winter shoulder months — demand is already trending up there, so incremental spend converts best.
  2. Use light-snow/rain days for fleet maintenance, not promotions — demand drops sharply regardless of marketing, making it the lowest-cost service window.
  3. Don't over-invest in weekday-specific promotions — there's a real Saturday bump but no broader weekday effect, so weekend-timed offers beat general day-of-week discounting.
  4. Plan reopening capacity on the growth trend, not last year's weather — the year-over-year effect suggests demand recovery will exceed prior-year levels.
